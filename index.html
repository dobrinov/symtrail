The pattern

Turn "he's always ill" into a chart

Some children spike a fever on something close to a schedule — the clinical name is PFAPA, periodic fever syndrome, and if that's where you've ended up searching, this is built for exactly that. Mark a child's profile and Symtrail groups the fever days into a single flare, works out the average gap between flares, and tells you the window when the next one is likely.

  • Automatic flare grouping from temperature and symptom entries
  • Average, shortest and longest gap between flares, updated every time
  • A predicted window, so you can plan a birthday party or a work trip around it

One diary per child

Each child's history stands on its own

Add a profile for each child — name, birth date, a PFAPA toggle if it applies — and switch between them in a tap. If you're tracking your own recurring migraines or fevers, add yourself too; it's the same diary, just one more person in it.

Questions

What people ask before they download it

Does this send my child's information anywhere?

No. Everything you log — symptoms, temperatures, doses, notes — stays on your phone and is never uploaded. We only see anonymous, coarse counts of which screens get used, never what you actually logged.

Do I need to make an account first?

No. There's no sign-up, no email, no password. Install it and start logging.

My son gets a fever every few weeks — is this what it's for?

Yes. Mark his profile as a PFAPA patient and Symtrail groups fever days into flares, works out the average gap between them, and shows the window when the next one is likely. It works just as well for any recurring pattern, whether or not it has a name yet.

What if I have no signal at the hospital?

Logging works fully offline — in a waiting room, a hospital corridor, or on a plane. Nothing about using Symtrail depends on a connection.

Can I track more than just my kids?

Yes. Symtrail keeps a separate diary per person, so alongside your children you can add yourself — useful if you're tracking your own recurring migraines or fevers too.

Will a doctor take a printout from an app seriously?

The report is your own observations laid out clearly — dates, temperatures, doses, flare patterns — not a diagnosis. It's built to make a five-minute appointment more useful, not to replace the conversation with your paediatrician.

Is Symtrail going to tell me what's wrong with my child?

No. Symtrail is a diary, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medical advice — it just helps you keep track of what you're already observing, and show it to someone qualified.
