What it does

Everything you'd otherwise scribble on the back of an envelope

Patterns, not guesswork

See the rhythm behind recurring fevers

Some fevers come back on a schedule. Symtrail groups related days into a single flare, measures the gap between them and shows you the window when the next one is likely — so you can plan around it instead of being ambushed.

  • Automatic flare grouping from your temperature and symptom logs
  • Average, shortest and longest gap between flares
  • A predicted window for the next onset, updated with every entry

Questions

Frequently asked

Is my health data private?

Yes. Everything you log stays on your phone. Symtrail doesn't require an account, and symptom names, temperatures and notes are never uploaded.

Can I track more than one person?

Yes. Symtrail is built around profiles, so you can keep a separate diary for each child, for a partner or parent you care for, and for yourself. Switching between people takes one tap.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging works with no signal at all — in a waiting room, on a plane, or at 3am in a hospital corridor.

Does Symtrail help with PFAPA?

Yes. Mark a profile as a PFAPA patient and Symtrail groups fevers into flares, works out the average gap between them, and shows the window when the next flare is likely. It's just as useful for any recurring or hard-to-describe symptom pattern.

What languages does it support?

Twenty, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech, Romanian, Bulgarian, Greek, Swedish, Turkish, Ukrainian, Russian, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. The app picks up your phone's language automatically, and dates follow it too.

How much does it cost?

Symtrail is free to download.

Is Symtrail a medical device?

No. It's a diary that helps you record and share what you observe. It doesn't diagnose anything and it isn't a substitute for medical advice.
